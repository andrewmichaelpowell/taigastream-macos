Taiga Stream (macOS)

Taiga Stream puts your favorite radio stations and podcasts within easy reach. With unique preset buttons available directly from the Control Center and Lock Screen, you can switch stations or podcasts instantly without interrupting what you're doing.

Create presets and enjoy quick access to the music, news, sports, and podcasts you love. With iCloud, your presets automatically sync across your Apple devices, giving you the same personalized listening experience everywhere.
